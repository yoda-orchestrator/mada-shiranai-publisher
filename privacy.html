Mada Shiranai Hanashi Publisher

Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 11, 2026

1. Application and controller

Mada Shiranai Hanashi Publisher ("Application") is operated by the owner of the Mada Shiranai Hanashi educational video channel ("Operator"). The Operator controls the personal data described in this policy.

2. Data processed

The Application processes TikTok authorization tokens; the authorized account's basic profile identifiers and display name; granted scopes; submitted video files, captions, and metadata; publication IDs, status, and public URLs; and basic video metrics including views, likes, comments, and shares.

3. Sources and purposes

Account and video data is provided by the Operator or returned by TikTok after authorization. It is used only to authenticate the connected account, publish content requested by the Operator, monitor publication status, troubleshoot publishing, and review content performance.

4. Legal basis and consent

Processing occurs at the Operator's request and with authorization granted through TikTok Login Kit. Authorization can be revoked at any time through TikTok account settings.

5. Sharing

Data is shared with TikTok only as necessary to perform the requested login, publishing, status, and analytics functions. Data is not sold, rented, used for third-party advertising, or shared with unrelated third parties.

6. Storage and security

Authorization tokens and operational records are stored in the Operator's access-controlled private environment. Reasonable technical measures are used to restrict access and protect credentials.

7. Retention and deletion

Authorization tokens are retained only while needed to operate the connected account. Publication records and metrics are retained only for editorial and operational review. The Operator may revoke TikTok access and delete local credentials and records at any time.

8. Requests and contact

Privacy questions, access requests, and deletion requests can be submitted through the public support and contact page. Requests will be reviewed and handled as required by applicable law.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated when the Application or applicable requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

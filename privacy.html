Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 10, 2026

Data processed

Mada Shiranai Hanashi Publisher processes authorization tokens, the authorized TikTok account's basic profile identifiers, submitted video content and metadata, publication status, public post URLs, and basic performance metrics such as views, likes, comments, and shares.

How data is used

Data is used only to authenticate the channel operator, publish content requested by the operator, monitor publication status, and review content performance. Data is not sold, rented, or used for advertising.

Storage and sharing

Credentials and operational records are stored in the operator's private environment. Data is shared only with TikTok as required to perform the requested publishing and analytics functions.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are retained only while needed to operate the authorized account. The operator may revoke access in TikTok settings and delete local credentials and records at any time.

Contact

Privacy questions and deletion requests may be sent through the official Mada Shiranai Hanashi TikTok account associated with this application.
